Field manual

How to play

Claim 80% of the field to clear a sector.

  1. Leave safe ground. Use arrow keys or WASD to steer the white diamond. On touchscreens, swipe or use the direction pad.
  2. Close the line. Crossing open space draws a trail. Reach cyan territory or the border to claim the area cut off from the Styx.
  3. Protect your trail. The Styx can cut any exposed line. Avoid crossing your own trail, and watch for gold crawlers along the edges.
