Vitrail

Cut the dark into panes of light.

  • Steer off the glowing line to start a cut. Reach any line again and the pane you sealed lights up.
  • The Wisp drifts in the dark. If it touches an unfinished cut, you lose a diamond.
  • Cinders run along the lines. Don't let one catch you, or reach the start of your cut.
  • Light 75% of the window to finish it.

Arrow keys or WASD. On a touch screen, swipe to steer and tap to stop.
